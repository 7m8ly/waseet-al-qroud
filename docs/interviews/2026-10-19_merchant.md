# Interview: merchant (Shorja market, Baghdad, 19 Oct 2026)
## Findings (paraphrased)
- Has a wallet that carries part of the income; has not considered a loan so far.
- Expects a lender to ask for property deeds as collateral, sellable on default.
- A good explanation answers every question fully and is backed by recorded electronic statements.
- Prefers a form to a chat because it gives time to think; writes Modern Standard Arabic.
- Dislikes wallet transactions being analysed; reassured when the wallet is officially licensed.
- Could not commit to any monthly installment: income is spent on living costs.
## Design changes
Form-first intake; MSA supported; consent with documents-only path; guarantee stated up front; "no installment fits" handled honestly.
