# Interview: loan officer (Jadriya, Baghdad, 20 Oct 2026)
## Findings (paraphrased)
- Files mostly lack documentation and data accuracy; chasing documents is quick because requirements are simple.
- Most common refusal reasons: no guarantee against the amount, and unclear feasibility or purpose.
- Evidence of activity for cash traders: lease contract, business licence, professional licence, purchase invoices, inspection report.
- Installment should not exceed 50% of income or net income; activity period 1 to 5 years depending on the amount.
- Wants full applicant data, amount, purpose and guarantees in the file.
- An automated tool alone is not enough; field visits are required.
## Design changes
E3 = 50% of net income; purpose and guarantee required; accepted-documents list; "field visit required" in every file; problem narrowed to accuracy, guarantees and purpose.
